# Code of Conduct

ZipZap welcomes contributors of every background and level of experience.
Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; explain concerns with evidence.
- Respect different experiences, identities, and communication styles.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and give credit for others' work.
- Protect personal information and respect a request to stop unwanted contact.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation,
personal attacks, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Use GitHub's private
[Report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
flow for the relevant content or account. For a non-sensitive moderation request,
contact a maintainer on the relevant project thread. The current maintainer is
listed in [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, explain a warning, temporarily
restrict participation, or ban repeated or severe misconduct. They should act
proportionately, keep private reports confidential within the limits of GitHub's
platform, and explain moderation decisions without exposing a reporter.

If a concern involves the maintainer, report it directly to GitHub rather than
to that maintainer. GitHub's own policies and enforcement remain applicable.
